# The first screen, against the best (herostudio, 2026-11-26)

What five products put on the first screen a signed-out visitor sees, read on
26 Nov 2026, and what brickworks.diy's does better. The page itself is
docs/HOME.md; the loop is `tools/site/hero_capture.py`.

## What each does

**BrickLink Studio** (bricklink.com/v3/studio/download.page). The headline
is one changing word and "in Studio" (Build, Render, Instructions), each
word timed to its part of a video (1 s, 12.2 s, 17.2 s); under it "Digital
brick building software, powered by the LEGO Group." One button, "Download
Studio for Windows" (or Mac, by the system). Beside it the product at work:
a video that plays itself, muted, inline, looping, with controls, 26 s at
1920 x 1080 in H.264 with a sound track, 21.5 MB, asked for as the page
mounts, with no poster, so the first screen shows an empty box until it
comes. No community on that screen. On a phone: "To download Studio,
please use a desktop computer." Its front page is "Brick building software,
powered by the LEGO Group.", Download today, a contest and staff picks.

**Mecabricks** (mecabricks.com). "Build & Render your 3D LEGO® models
online" (not a heading), "No plug-in, no installation…", Start Now ! with
Log In and Sign Up in the header. One still render (a 180 KB JPEG) under a
logo banner, a Twitter embed and an advertisement; "Discover thousands of
models" with a grid under it. No viewport tag: a phone is shown the
desktop page shrunk.

**Rebrickable** (rebrickable.com). An eyebrow "Build more. Buy fewer
bricks.", the headline "Build something new with the LEGO® bricks you
already own" and "Add your collection and Rebrickable will show you which
fan-designed creations you can build today." Two buttons, Explore MOCs and
Add my collection, and a real count ("more than 206,000 MOCs"). One still
photograph of a member's model, then the grid. On a phone the words
centred, the two buttons one over the other.

**Figma** (figma.com). "Build with the full-stack creative canvas", no line
under it; Get started, with Log in, Contact sales and Get started for free
in the header. A carousel of eight clips of prompts becoming designs, each
with a PNG poster (140 KB to 1.18 MB), a visible pause and play, previous
and next; it keeps still for somebody who asked for less motion. Customers'
logos next. No community.

**Linear** (linear.app). "The product development system for teams and
agents", "Purpose-built for planning and building products. Designed for
the AI era." and a link to what is new; no buttons in the hero (Log in and
Sign up in the header). The product's real interface built in the page's
own HTML (its sidebar, an issue's thread with an agent's pull request),
fading in from a blur. On a phone the headline breaks into four lines.

## What we took

- From Linear, the real product, alive, as the picture: here the studio
  itself, recorded from the app, not a mock.
- From Studio, words timed to the picture's beats: captions over the loop
  as it comes to each ("Drop a brick on the studs", "Follow the steps",
  "Render it like a photo").
- From Figma, a poster first, a pause anybody can find, and stillness for
  somebody who asked for less motion.
- From Rebrickable, two clear actions and the community's real builds
  right under them.
- From Mecabricks, saying there is nothing to install.

## What ours does better

- **You can build from the first screen.** Start building, free opens the
  studio in the browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone, with nothing
  to download (a free account, signed in with a code we email). Studio's
  first screen is a download; its phone page turns a phone away.
- **The whole story in one loop.** A little house built brick by brick
  onto its studs, a model's steps played, the model rendered like a
  photograph, in under 20 seconds; Studio tells the same three in 26 s,
  Figma and Linear one each, Mecabricks and Rebrickable none (one still
  each). It ends on the finished picture, never on a form.
- **Light.** Each cut is about 1 MB or less (AV1, with H.264 for a browser
  without it), asked for only after the page's load, with a sharp poster
  drawn first; Studio's 21.5 MB video, with sound, starts as the page does
  and shows nothing until it comes. Somebody saving data, or asking for less
  motion, is never sent ours at all.
- **A phone gets its own.** A 9:16 cut of the phone studio, picked by the
  width; Studio sends phones away and Mecabricks shrinks its desktop page.
- **The product and its community on one screen.** Real builds from the
  feed begin just under the fold, each one open-able, remixable and
  buildable in place; of the five only Rebrickable shows members' work so
  soon, and none lets you open it in the tool from there.
- **In four languages**, its captions too: German, French and Spanish as
  well as English.
