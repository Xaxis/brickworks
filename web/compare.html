Studio exists. Why Brickworks?

BrickLink Studio is the community’s standard, and it does a lot well: parts from the BrickLink catalogue, photoreal renders, an instruction maker. LEGO® Digital Designer was retired by the LEGO Group in 2022. Here is Brickworks beside them, each point shown in the app on a real model.

  1. In a browser, nothing to install

    Open brickworks.diy/app on any computer and build. Every tab is its own project. Desktop apps too, if you prefer.

    Studio is a desktop app for Windows and Mac.

  2. Will it stand? Will it hold?

    Check stability weighs each joint against what its studs can hold, and the centre of mass against the base, in every pose the hinges reach.

    Studio’s check flags weak parts and groups that can’t stand, and calls itself “very conservative”. LDD had none.

  3. Gear trains turn at their real ratios

    Simulate turns the crank and every gear follows. This orrery keeps all eight planets’ years within 0.35% of NASA’s.

    Studio’s animations build the model or circle the camera.

  4. Your Studio files, in and out

    Open a Studio .io, locked ones too, and save back to .io. LDD’s .lxf opens as well.

  5. STL, OBJ and GLB

    True to size, for a 3D printer, Blender or the web.

    Studio’s 3D exports are COLLADA and POV-Ray.

  6. Instructions in one press

    A booklet with the parts for every step, laid out for you and ready to print.

And an assistant, only if you want one

Claude, on your own plan or key, checks the physics and designs on request. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, Assistant. How it works
