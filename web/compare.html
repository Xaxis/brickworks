Studio exists. Why Brickworks?

BrickLink Studio is the community’s standard, and it does a lot well: parts from the BrickLink catalogue, photoreal renders, an instruction maker. Mecabricks, LeoCAD and LDCad have strengths of their own. Here is Brickworks beside them: six points shown in the app on real models, then the whole field, row by row, and where the others still lead.

Open the app See the whole field

  1. In a browser, nothing to install

    Open brickworks.diy/app on any computer, or on a tablet or a phone, laid out for a finger. Every tab is its own project, and your models follow you to every device you sign in on. Desktop apps too.

    Studio is a desktop app for Windows and Mac.

  2. Will it stand? Will it hold?

    Check stability weighs each joint against what its studs can hold, and the centre of mass against the base, in every pose the hinges reach. View › Stress lists the weakest joints, worst first, and says what would fix each.

    Studio’s check flags weak parts and groups that can’t stand, and calls itself “very conservative”. LDD had none.

  3. Gear trains turn at their real ratios

    A gear put down by hand seats in mesh with the one beside it. Simulate turns the crank and every gear follows: this orrery keeps all eight planets’ years within 0.35% of NASA’s.

    Studio’s animations build the model or circle the camera. LDCad turns a mechanism by a script you write.

  4. Your Studio files, in and out

    Open a Studio .io, locked ones too, with the parts only Studio has, and save back to .io. LDD’s .lxf opens as well.

    An .io saved here has not yet been tried in Studio itself.

  5. STL, OBJ and GLB

    True to size, for a 3D printer, Blender or the web.

    Studio’s 3D exports are COLLADA and POV-Ray.

  6. Instructions in one press

    A booklet with the parts for every step, laid out for you, then by hand if you like: each step’s view, the page breaks, callouts, new parts outlined. Print it, or save it as a PDF or as PNG pages.

    Studio’s Instruction Maker lays out pages and saves them as PDF and PNG too.

Where the others still lead

Renders. Studio’s Eyesight is a path tracer, and Mecabricks has a render farm and a Blender add-on. Render here builds a picture from frames drawn in real time; for a path-traced one, export a GLB to Blender.

Prices. Studio is BrickLink’s own app: it shows BrickLink’s prices and puts a model on a wanted list in one click. Brickworks writes the wanted list and the Rebrickable list, with no prices.

Rubber bands and springs. LDCad shapes a rubber band round the circles it passes, and builds springs and shock absorbers. Brickworks lays hoses, chains and tread, not bands or springs.

Years. Studio has years of users, a tutorial at first launch and official videos. Brickworks has an eight-card tour.

And an assistant, only if you want one

Your own AI, Claude on your plan or Claude or another AI on your own key, checks the physics and designs on request, never limited on any plan. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, Assistant. How it works
