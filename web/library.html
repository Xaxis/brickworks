Where these come from

Every set’s facts, parts list and picture are Rebrickable’s, the pictures kept here so your browser never asks them, and its parts are drawn by Brickworks. Those marked 3D are pictured as built: models somebody built in LDraw, piece by piece, for LDraw’s Official Model Repository, and shared under a Creative Commons licence that lets anyone use them as long as they credit them: each card and page names who modelled it and links to its page there. In the app, the 3D ones are File › Library…; a set opened from it keeps its credit when you share it.
