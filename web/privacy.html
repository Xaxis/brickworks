Privacy

Your account is your email address, and that is nearly all there is of you here: the address, when you last signed in, which Claude apps you connected, and for a day the network address a sign-in code was asked from, so the form cannot be used to flood an inbox.

Your email address

Your account. You sign in with a code we email you and stay signed in on that browser or computer until you sign out. It is used to sign you in and for nothing else.

Your API key

Kept in your browser, or for this visit only, and sent only to Anthropic. The page’s security policy lets it talk to itself, Anthropic, Wikimedia for reference pictures, Rebrickable, its own parts store and sign-in service, and the visit counter below, and nothing else.

Your Claude login

Never asked for. Claude signs in to Brickworks as you, with your email code and your Allow, and holds a Brickworks token, never the other way round. Disconnect Claude under Account ends it everywhere at once. That you connected Claude, and when, is kept past it, so connecting again sends you to the Brickworks you already have in Claude.

Your conversations

A small relay carries Claude’s tool calls to your open tab and the answers back, and deletes each message the moment it is answered. Claude’s tokens are kept only as hashes.

Your models

Saved in your browser or on your computer, not on a server. A model is put online only when you share it by link, and taken down when you say.

Your visit

We count visits with our own privacy-friendly analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; your IP address is used only to make a daily anonymous count and is never stored. To opt out on this site, open any page with ?ov_ignore=1. Shared models, people’s pages and signing in are never counted.

Every line of it is on GitHub, so none of this has to be taken on trust. Updated 16 October 2026.
