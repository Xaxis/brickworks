Looking for your sign-in…

Sign in to choose what Brickworks emails you, what your profile shows and which Claude apps can work in your tabs. We email you a code: no password.

Public profiles and public collections are for people 13 and over, so this account keeps its collections and favourites to itself. Building, your models and sharing them by link are all yours.

Appearance

System follows your computer’s light or dark setting. Kept in this browser, and on your account, so your other browsers follow it once you sign in.

Email

Brickworks emails you only what you ask for here, from the address sign-in codes come from. Off until you turn it on.

Privacy

Brickworks asks everyone once whether they are 13 or over, and keeps only that answer.

Your profile shows how many people follow you and how many you follow, never who. Nobody is told you followed them, and following is not a way to write to anybody.

A model shows how many people liked it, never who. You may show on your profile the models you liked, as a tab of their cards; off until you turn it on.

Claude

Whether your Claude can work in your Brickworks tabs.

    How to connect

    Sign out

    This browser forgets its sign-in. Your account and everything in it stay.

    Delete this account…

    Your models come out of the gallery and their links stop working; your profile, favourites, collections, comments, follows, photos, the sets you own and your Claude connections are deleted with the account. It cannot be undone.
