Privacy

What is counted

Brickworks counts how its site and its studio are used, so it can tell what helps people build and what gets in their way. It counts acts, never what they hold: that a model was saved, never the model.

Updated 18 November 2026

On this page

  • What is counted
  • What is never counted
  • When you are asked
  • Turning it off
  • How long it is kept
  • Who sees it

What is counted

On the site: which kinds of page are opened (a model’s page, a profile, a challenge, never which one), where the visit came from (the site that linked here, and a campaign’s tag in the link), signing in, and acts such as a like, a comment, a follow or an entry, by their name only.

In the studio: which tools, panels, dialogs and buttons are used and for how long, which commands are run from the command palette, that a part was put down and a model saved (how many, never which), how long the first part and the first save took, how long Simulate, Render, booklets and Building together ran and whether they worked, and the last thing done before leaving.

Everywhere: how long pages and the studio take to load and how smoothly they draw, where an error happened (a file and a line, never its words), the kind of device, browser and system, and the site’s language. For your Claude’s tool calls, which tool, whether it worked and how long it took, never whose account made it.

A visit is a random number kept in the tab until it closes, or until it has been away for 30 minutes. Signed in, your account is kept with your visits as a code made from it with a secret key, so we can tell how many builders come back, never who they are.

What is never counted

Your models or anything in them, a part’s number, what you type, a file’s name, your email address and your IP address. The country your connection comes from is read only to know whether to ask first, and is not kept. Nothing is sent to anybody else: the counts are kept in Brickworks’ own database.

When you are asked

In the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, nothing is counted until you say yes to the question at the foot of a page. Elsewhere it is counted unless you turn it off. A browser that sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is never counted, and is never asked.

Turning it off

Untick Count how I use Brickworks in this browser in Settings, under Privacy, or answer No to the question. Nothing is sent from that browser from then on. It is a choice for each browser, signed in or not.

How long it is kept

Each count as it was sent is kept for 30 days and then deleted. What is kept after that is each day’s totals (how many visits, how many people, how often something was used), which hold nothing about any one visit. Which days a builder came back, and when they first put down a part and saved, are kept with the account’s code, and deleted when the account is.

Who sees it

Only the owner of Brickworks, in the site’s own console. The visit counter described in the privacy policy is a separate one.

Questions about any of it: open an issue on GitHub.
