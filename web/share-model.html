Share a model you made

Made it in BrickLink Studio, LDCad, MLCad or another LDraw program? Choose its file, and in a minute it has a page of its own: its parts list and its steps read from the file, its picture drawn here.

Your model’s file

Or drop it on this page. An LDraw file (.ldr, .mpd or .dat) or a BrickLink Studio file (.io): it is read in your browser, and nothing is sent until you share it.

LEGO® Digital Designer files open in the app

The app turns an LDD model’s parts into LDraw’s. Open it there with File › Open…, then share it with File › Share….

About it

Anyone may share it, change it and build on it, crediting you.

Its link works either way. What is listed follows the guidelines.

Anyone with its link can download the file itself, with what it says inside. An email address in its Author line, and folders on your computer, are left out.
