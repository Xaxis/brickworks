First launch
  1. Open the zip and drag Brickworks into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it cannot check it for malicious software: choose Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Under Security, choose Open Anyway beside Brickworks, then Open.

macOS asks because Apple has not notarised this build yet. It asks only the first time.

If it says the app is damaged, that is the download's quarantine flag, not damage. Verify the download, then in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brickworks.app

  1. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. It will not run from inside the zip.
  2. Open the folder and run Brickworks.exe.
  3. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. It asks only the first time, because this build is not signed yet.

In your file manager, open the AppImage's Properties and allow it to run as a program. Then double-click it. Or in a terminal:

chmod +x Brickworks-*.AppImage && ./Brickworks-*.AppImage

Will not start? Your system may lack FUSE. Get the .tar.xz instead, and run it from where it unpacks:

tar -xf Brickworks-*.tar.xz && ./Brickworks-*/brickworks.x86_64

Verify the download

Every file's SHA-256 is in SHA256SUMS on the release on GitHub.

What is new

  • Every dialog and button in the app's own look.
  • One part count for a model, wherever it is shown.
  • Stress weighs a Technic model's pins and axles.
  • Find a technique by its words, a part by its number.
  • Build a model together with someone, live.
  • Works with a screen reader and the keyboard alone.
  • Find a part by its BrickLink name, its number, or a photo.
  • Save a booklet as a PDF.
  • Your models kept with your account, on every device.
  • Every export asks where to save it.
  • Big booklets are drawn about six times faster.
  • Gears mesh and doors hang as you put them down.
  • View, Stress shows where a model would give.
  • Big models open without freezing the window.
  • Moving parts turn by hand more smoothly.
  • Claude finds parts by a builder's own words.
  • Open any of 1,575 real set models from the Library.
  • 85 sets steer as the real ones do.
  • Claude designs twice and keeps the better.
  • Brickify lays steep sides on their sides.
  • Motions: name how a model moves, play it, and film it.
  • Racks, cranks and sliders move as the real parts do.
  • Brickify reads PLY, 3MF, VOX and FBX files.
  • A second model in a tab of its own.
  • Instruction booklets, with steps that build as drawn.
  • Render your model, share it by link, pose and animate it.
  • Turn a 3D file into bricks, or a picture into Brick Art.
  • Export to Studio, BrickLink, Rebrickable or a 3D program.
