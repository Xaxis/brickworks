        How to tell us

        Help, Send feedback… in the app, or Send feedback here: what happened, and what you expected. Add a picture of what you see, and in the app tick the model and the log, which tell us what it was doing. Your reports are marked as a tester's, and each one, with where it stands and our answer, is on your settings page.
