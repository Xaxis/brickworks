Have an account? You keep everything

Every account made before 29 October 2026, when this page changed, gets Pro, free and with no end date: the desktop app, every version of it, and every model kept with your account. There is nothing to do: it is on your account when Pro opens.

Education is free

Students and teachers can have Pro free, a year at a time and again when you ask, with a school email or a short form once Pro opens; for a school, the same for each teacher. Teaching? Say so in the form’s line.

What stays free

Pro adds the desktop app and every model kept with your account. It takes nothing out of Free, which keeps all of this:

  • The web app. In any browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone.
  • AI and building by hand, without a limit. Bring your own AI: Claude or ChatGPT through the connector, Claude Code, or an API key of your own.
  • Everything else the app does today. Saving to your device, 25 models kept with your account, Build together, booklets and PDFs, renders, exports, photo search, parts lists, publishing, the gallery and challenges.
  • Your models. Nothing is ever deleted or locked: on any plan, or none, the app opens, edits and exports everything you made. With more than 25 kept with your account you keep every one; a new one goes there once you are under 25 again.
  • What you downloaded. Every desktop version you download keeps working. Until Pro opens, the desktop app is free to download.

Questions

This page said the desktop app was free. What changed?

Until 29 October 2026 it said Free kept everything, the desktop app included. The plan changed: Free is the web app, with AI without a limit and 25 models kept with your account, and the desktop app and every model kept with your account come with Pro. Everyone who had an account before then keeps both, free, with no end date. Nothing anyone has today is taken away.

Is AI limited on Free?

No. AI has no limit on any plan, because you bring your own: Claude or ChatGPT through the connector, Claude Code, or your own API key for any provider. Brickworks never meters it or pays for its tokens.

Why Pro at all?

To pay for what Brickworks costs to run and to make: the servers the app and the connector run on, the storage your models are kept in, and a desktop app built and tested for three systems. The AI itself runs on your own plan or key, so Brickworks never sells model use or takes a cut of it.

Can I pay now?

No. Pro is not on sale yet, and nothing here takes a card. When it opens, its price, how it renews and how to cancel are said here and in the terms before you pay.

Yes: choose Monthly or Yearly, then Upgrade to Pro. Before you pay, the next step says the price, that it renews until you cancel and how to cancel, as the terms do.

What if I stop paying for Pro?

Nothing stops. Every desktop version released while you paid stays yours and keeps working, online or off, and your account goes back to Free: the web app, and 25 models kept with your account. Every model already there stays; a new one goes there once you are under 25. Nothing you made is deleted or locked.

What does the form keep?

Your email, monthly or yearly, and your line, only to write to you once when Pro opens. To take it off, open the form, type the address and choose take it off. More in privacy.
