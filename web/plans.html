Plans · coming soon

Free, for good. Two plans are coming.

Everything Brickworks does today stays free. Plus and Creator are for more room on the server, and for selling what you make. Nothing here takes money yet: say which you would use, and we will write once, when it opens.

Free

Everything Brickworks does today.

$0 for good

Open the app

What it has:

  • The web app, and the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • The desktop works offline, every part on disk
  • The whole LDraw parts library, and its official models of real sets
  • Physics: whether it stands, and joints that move
  • Booklets to print or save as PDF
  • Find a part from a photo
  • Parts lists for Rebrickable and BrickLink
  • Design with AI on your own Claude
  • Publish to the gallery
  • Build together, live, by a link
  • Your models kept with your account, up to 500, and every version of a model you share

Plus

Coming soon

More room on the server.

$48 a year, or $5 a month

Everything in Free. Coming with Plus:

  • Unlimited private models in the cloud
  • Full version history of the models kept with your account
  • Build together for up to 8
  • Early-access builds
  • A supporter badge

Creator

Coming soon

For people who sell what they make.

$96 a year, or $10 a month

Everything in Plus. Coming with Creator:

  • A commercial-use licence: sell instructions, kits or commissioned builds, or use Brickworks in a business
  • Build together for up to 20
  • Priority support
  • Later: sell instructions from a model’s page at a 5% fee (10% on Free and Plus)

Education is free

Students and teachers will have Plus free, with a school email or a short form, and schools free accounts. It comes with the plans. Teaching? Choose Plus in the form and say so in its line.

What Free keeps, for good

  • The app is free. In your browser, on a tablet or a phone, and on the desktop.
  • It works offline. The desktop app has every part on disk.
  • Nothing it does today will be taken away. What Free lists above stays in Free.
  • It never locks. Offline, or with a plan that has ended, the app opens, edits and exports everything; only what the server does for a plan pauses. Over a plan’s limit, models become read-only, never deleted.

Questions

Can I pay now?

No. There is no checkout, and nothing here takes a card. This page asks who would use a plan before any is built.

Why have plans at all?

To pay for what costs money to run, and to share in what makes you money: storage, history and bigger rooms for Build together on the server, and selling what you make. Never for the app itself.

What does the form keep?

Your email, the plan, monthly or yearly, and your line, only to write to you once when that plan opens. To take it off, open the form, type the address and choose take it off. More in privacy.

Tell me when it’s ready

One email when the plan opens. Nothing is charged, and nothing else is sent.

Changed your mind? Type the address and , from every plan.
