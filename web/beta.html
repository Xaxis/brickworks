Test Brickworks Pro for a month, free

We are looking for 100 people to use Brickworks for real and tell us honestly how it goes. Each tester gets 30 days of Pro, free: no card, nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.

Apply →What Pro is

What testers get

30 days of Pro on your account, from the day we approve you, or from the day Pro launches if that is later.

  • Every model kept

    Keep as many models with your account as you like, on every device you sign in to. Free keeps 25.

  • The desktop app

    Brickworks for Windows, macOS and Linux, with every part on disk. It works with your AI exactly as the web app does, and with Claude Code too.

  • Your own AI, as always

    You bring your own: Claude or ChatGPT through the connector, Claude Code, or your own key. Brickworks never counts it, on Free or on Pro.

What we ask

Nothing to pay, and not much of your time.

  1. Use it for real

    Build things you want to build, in your browser and on the desktop, over the month.

  2. Tell us honestly

    What works, what does not, and what you looked for and could not find. We ask by email, a question or two.

  3. That is all

    No card, no subscription, nothing to cancel. When the month ends, it ends.

When the month ends

You are back on Free. Nothing is charged, no card is asked for, and nothing is deleted. Above 25 models kept with your account, you keep every one of them and can open, change and save them all, but you add no new ones until you are under 25.

Had an account before 29 October 2026?

It has Pro already, free and with no end date (Pricing). You are welcome as a tester all the same: apply, and you will not take one of the 100 spots.

Questions

Who can apply?

Anyone with a Brickworks account who signs in with an email address, since the beta writes to you. One application an account.

How are testers chosen?

We read every application ourselves. The first 100 we approve get the spots, numbered in the order we approve them. Nothing is decided by a machine.

Is the month a subscription?

No. It is 30 days of Pro given to your account, once. Nothing renews, nothing is charged, and no card is ever asked for. To keep Pro after it, you choose to, on Pricing.

What if the 100 spots are gone?

Applications stay open, as a waitlist. If we open more spots, the waitlist is read first.

What if I am not chosen?

We email you, and you can apply again 30 days later. Brickworks stays free in your browser either way.

What happens to what I send?

It is kept with your account, read only by the site’s owner, and deleted with your account. An application we decline is erased 90 days after. No IP address is kept. More in privacy.
