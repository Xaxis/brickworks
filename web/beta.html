What testers get

Nothing to pay, and not much of your time: use it for real and tell us honestly how it goes.

  • New things first

    Early features on your account before everyone, and each week’s changes here, as they go out.

  • A month of Pro, free

    The first 100 testers we approve get 30 days of Pro: every model kept with your account (Free keeps 25), and commercial use. No card, and nothing to cancel.

  • Heard and answered

    Every report is read by the site’s owner. Yours are in your account, each with where it stands and our answer.

Report a bug in one press

From wherever you are, without leaving what you were doing.

  1. Press Report a bug

    In the app, at the top beside Account, or in Help. On the site, in the bar at the foot of every page.

  2. Mark the spot

    A picture of what you see is taken for you. Mark where it went wrong, and say what you expected.

  3. See what we do

    It says what will be sent before it goes. After, it is in Your reports, with where it stands and our answer.

      Early features on your account

        Tell us about you

        For a month of Pro: the first 100 testers we approve get 30 days, free. It takes a minute, and it’s up to you.

        What you send is kept with your account, read only by the site’s owner, and deleted with your account (privacy).

        Questions

        Who can join?

        Anyone with a Brickworks account, while the beta is open. Press Join and you are a tester at once. New to Brickworks? Signing up takes your email and a code, no password.

        Is the month of Pro for every tester?

        For the first 100 we approve. We read who joins ourselves; nothing is decided by a machine. Each gets 30 days of Pro, once, from the day we approve them, or from the day Pro launches if that is later. Joining never waits on it.

        What happens when the month ends?

        You are back on Free. Nothing is charged, no card is asked for, and nothing is deleted. Above 25 models kept with your account, you keep every one of them and can open, change and save them all, but you add no new ones until you are under 25.

        Had an account before 29 October 2026?

        It has Pro already, free and with no end date (Plans). You are welcome as a tester all the same, and you take none of the 100 months.

        What happens to what I send?

        A report is kept with your account and read only by the site’s owner. Only what you choose goes with it, and you see it before it goes. It is deleted with your account. More in privacy.
