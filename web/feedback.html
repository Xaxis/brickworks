What goes with it

Only what you choose, and you see it before it goes. On the site: a picture of the page, or one you choose, and with a tick, the page's address, your window's size and your browser's name.

In the app, Send feedback… in the Brickworks menu (top left) also offers, each with its own tick: the app's version and your computer's system, the model you have open as a file, and the app's last log lines, which say what it was doing. A picture of the app's window is yours to add or take off.

Where it goes

To the people who make Brickworks, and nobody else. Signed in, your reports are on your settings page, with where each stands and our answers. Signed out, we answer at the address you give. What we keep, and for how long.

Beta testers

A tester's reports are marked as a tester's, so we read them first. The beta hub has what to try and what we already know is wrong.
