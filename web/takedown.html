Takedown requests

Something here is yours, or should not be

The gallery shows what people chose to share. Brickworks does not check each model before it is listed, so tell us when one should not be there, and the site's owner takes it out.

The quick way

Every model's page, every profile and every collection has a Report button. Choose what is wrong and send it; you do not need an account.

If it is your work, or your mark

Give the page's address, say what is yours and how, and how to reach you. While we look, we may take it out of the gallery; once we agree, it stays out, and the same model published again cannot be listed. Its own link is its sharer's to take down; we tell them.

If your model was taken out by mistake

When a model comes out of the gallery because somebody said it was theirs, and you made it or have the right to share it, send a counter-notice. We pass it on to whoever asked for the takedown. Unless they tell us within ten to fourteen working days that they have gone to court over it, it can be listed again.

Brickworks' copyright agent

The forms on this page reach the site's owner, who answers notices and counter-notices.

Anything else

A problem with the app itself goes to GitHub, where it is answered in the open.
