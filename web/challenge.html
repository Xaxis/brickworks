The rules

    No rules but the brief.

    Brickworks checks every rule on the model as it was shared, when it is entered, and again while the challenge runs: an entry whose newer version breaks one says so, and is not counted.

    How to enter

    Share a model in the gallery with your name on it, then on its page press Enter this month’s challenge and say whether you built it by hand or with the assistant: the two are judged apart. Up to three of yours. People signed in and 13 or over vote, once in each group; the votes are counted when it ends, and the most voted in each group wins.
