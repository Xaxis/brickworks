What it does

Everything here is built on the exact engine rather than on an approximation of it.

Build by hand

Point at a face and the part goes against it. Positions are whole numbers on a 2 LDU lattice, so a model saved today loads identically tomorrow.

Every part, findable

All 28,319, searchable as you type, previewed from the real geometry in the colour you picked.

A design assistant

Describe something and watch it appear. Every placement is checked against the real lattice, and you get the brick and the reason when one will not hold.

Will it stand up?

Live checks for a joint carrying more than its studs hold, a load hanging past its footprint, a build too tall for its base.

Yours to take away

Saved as LDraw .ldr, so it opens in LDView, LeoCAD, Studio or Mecabricks — and theirs opens here.

Build it for real

A parts list by the lot, which is how a shop sells them, and build steps that print as one page — because you build on a table, not at a screen.

A photograph, in plates

Drop in a picture, get a mosaic in the colours a 1 × 1 plate is really made in — a model like any other, parts list and instructions and all.

Real connections

Studs, tubes, Technic pin holes and axle holes, read from the primitives each part is drawn with rather than guessed from a bounding box.

Designed from one sentence

“A medieval castle with a gatehouse, four corner towers and a long curtain wall between them.” The assistant laid it out, checked every brick against the real lattice, then went back over the gatehouse, each tower and each wall on its own, close up, reaching for the parts real castle sets are built from.

Measured, not approximated

1 LDU = 0.4 mm exactly. Every dimension was read off the part geometry rather than copied from a reference, and the tests assert them, so a library update cannot move them quietly.

Sizes are nominal rather than as-moulded, which is what makes parts tile exactly instead of leaving a gap that accumulates across a long wall. The stud height is the LDraw library's own convention, and every part follows it.

Tiers

Building is free and always will be. The assistant costs real money to run and there is no payment system here yet, so it runs on a key of your own — which goes from your machine straight to Anthropic and never to us.
