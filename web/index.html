A complete CAD for LEGO® models

In any browser, with nothing to install. It checks that your model stands and which joints would fail, turns gear trains at their real ratios, opens and saves Studio .io, and exports 3D, renders and booklets.

The assistant

An engineer’s assistant, when you want one

Off until you turn it on in Settings, Assistant: build by hand and there is no AI anywhere in the window. On, it is Claude, on your own plan or API key.

  • Checks the physics of what you built and finds the failure points
  • Suggests a stronger build where a joint will not hold
  • Designs on request, from a sentence or a picture, sketch first
  • Edits a selection when you say what to change
  • Works from your own Claude over MCP. How to connect
