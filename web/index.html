How it works

From the first brick to the booklet

Your first model

  1. Find the part

    Any of LDraw’s parts, by its BrickLink or Rebrickable name, an element number, or a photo of the real one.

  2. Put it where it goes

    Studs, pins, axles and clips join where the real ones do, and nothing goes through anything. Turn a part by quarter turns, to the degree, or to any angle you type.

  3. Check it, then build it

    See where it would tip and which joints would give, set out the steps, then print the booklet or take the parts list to BrickLink or Rebrickable.

Built for builds that have to work

How it compares

  • Your Studio files

    Open BrickLink Studio .io files, with the parts only Studio has, and LDraw and LEGO® Digital Designer ones.

  • Grab it like the real thing

    A boom swings on its hinges, steering turns on its rack, an actuator stretches.

  • Build together, live

    One model built by several people at once, by a link, each seeing the others’ parts go down.

  • Renders and 3D export

    Renders up to 4K, 3D export at true size, and parts lists for BrickLink and Rebrickable.

  • Big models, in sections

    Sub-assemblies, copies that change with their original, and sections, as big sets are built.

  • Phones and tablets too

    The whole builder, laid out for a finger, and your models on every device you sign in on.

And minifigures, prints and stickers, hoses and chains, mosaics, parts made to any size, and what your own sets can build. All of it, in the docs

Optional

Bring your own assistant

Brickworks is made for building by hand. If you want help, turn on your own Claude: it can put a model together from a sentence, change the parts you select, or answer a question about it.

How the assistant works How to bring your Claude
  • Off until you turn it on. Off, there is no AI anywhere in the window.
  • Held to the same checks. It builds with the same real parts, and the app decides whether each one fits and holds.
  • You stay in charge. Change anything it puts down; a whole design is one Undo.
  • Never limited. It runs on your own Claude plan or key, never billed by Brickworks.
