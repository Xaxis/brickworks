Design LEGO® models with AI, in your browser

How it works

From a sentence to a model you can build

Designing with AI

  1. Say what you want

    “A red fire engine with a ladder that lifts.” Type it in the app’s assistant, or ask Claude in claude.ai and it builds in your open tab.

  2. Watch it build, part by part

    Each part goes down as the AI places it, every one from the LDraw library at true size. The app decides whether each part fits and holds, so what it builds is buildable.

  3. Make it yours, then build it

    Change anything by hand, check that it stands, then print the booklet, take the parts list to BrickLink or Rebrickable, or share it to the gallery.

The AI designer

It looks again, with fresh eyes

Every new design gets a stranger’s look at its first save: three pictures of the model and your own words. What does it read as? What reads as a generic box? Then the three changes a set designer would make first, made.

Judged against the same designs without it, designs with fresh eyes were preferred in two of every three verdicts.

Claude, or another AI on your own key, builds with the same real parts and checks you do. Off, there is no AI anywhere in the window.

In the app

How it compares

  • Every part, at true size

    All of LDraw’s library, found by its BrickLink or Rebrickable name, an element number or a photo. Pins, axles and studs join where the real ones do.

  • Your Studio files, both ways

    Open and save BrickLink Studio .io and LDraw. LEGO® Digital Designer files open too.

  • Grab it like the real thing

    A boom swings on its hinges, steering turns on its rack, an actuator stretches.

  • Build together, live

    One model built by several people at once, by a link, each seeing the others’ parts go down.

  • Renders and 3D export

    Renders up to 4K, 3D export at true size, and parts lists for BrickLink and Rebrickable.

  • On every device

    Your models on every device you sign in on, and what your own sets can build.
