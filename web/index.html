What’s new

Joining agrees to the beta’s terms and to emails about the beta. Leave any time in Settings.

  • New things first

    Early features on your account, and each week’s changes on the beta’s page.

  • A bug in one press

    Report a bug from any page or in the app. The picture is taken for you, and you mark the spot.

  • Heard and answered

    We read every report. Your account shows where each one stands, and our answer.

The studio

A CAD for LEGO® models, in your browser

Every LDraw part, joined where the real ones join. Check that it stands, print its booklet and share it with other builders.

How it compares with Studio

  • Will it stand?

    See where it would tip, which joints would give, and what would fix each.

  • Instructions and parts lists

    Print a booklet like the one in the box, or take the parts list to BrickLink or Rebrickable.

  • Real sets, ready to open

    Every LEGO® set has a page with its parts. Those the LDraw community has modelled open whole.

    Browse the sets

  • Free, in any browser

    The whole builder runs in any browser, a phone’s too, with nothing to install. A desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux is coming soon.

  • Your own AI, if you want it
