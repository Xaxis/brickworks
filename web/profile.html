Account

Your Brickworks account

Publish your models, keep favourites, follow designers and post photos of what you build. We email you a code to sign in: no password.

The same account signs you in to the app, in the browser and on the desktop.

Public profiles and public collections are for people 13 and over, so this account keeps its collections and favourites to itself. Building, your models and sharing them by link are all yours.

Make your profile

A name and a handle, and your models in the gallery have a page of their own. The rest (a few words, links, the models you pin) can wait for Edit profile.
