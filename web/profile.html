      Settings

      How the gallery shows you

      Your models are on your profile when you list them and tick Show who made it; a model shared without a name stays without one.

      What your profile shows

      Up to two links of yours elsewhere, one of your models’ pictures across the top, and up to three of your models pinned above the rest. Anyone can see them.

      Privacy

      Brickworks asks everyone once whether they are 13 or over, and keeps only that answer.

      Your profile shows how many people follow you and how many you follow, never who. Nobody is told you followed them, and following is not a way to write to anybody.

      Theme

      System follows your computer’s light or dark setting. Kept in this browser, and on your account, so your other browsers follow it once you sign in.

        How to connect

        Sign out

        This browser forgets its sign-in. Your account and everything in it stay.

        Delete this account…

        Your models come out of the gallery and their links stop working; your favourites, collections, comments, follows, photos, the sets you own and your Claude connections are deleted with the account. It cannot be undone.
