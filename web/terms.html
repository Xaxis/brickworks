Terms of use

Brickworks is a free CAD for LEGO® models: this website, the app in your browser and on your computer, and a gallery of what people share. Using any of it means these terms. They say what the site does and what it asks of you, in plain words.

To be filled in by the site’s owner: brickworks.diy is run by [the operator’s legal name]. Write to [a contact address] about anything on this page.

Your account

An account is your email address. You sign in with a code we email you; there is no password, so whoever reads your inbox can sign in as you. Keep it to yourself. One person, one account.

Your age

At your first sign-in you are asked when you were born. Brickworks keeps only whether you are 13 or over, and when you said so, never the date. Under 13, you can build, keep your models and share them by link like anyone. The gallery, a public profile, a name shown on a link, and comments and photos when they come, are for people 13 and over.

Your models

What you build is yours. It is saved in your browser or on your computer, not on our server, until you publish it. When you do, you choose a licence for it (the three are here): that says what others may do with it, and you keep the copyright.

Publishing lets Brickworks keep a copy, show it at its link and, if you list it, in the gallery; make its pictures, steps, booklet and parts list; and let others open a copy in their own Brickworks as its licence allows. Unpublish takes the copy down. Publish only what you have the right to share.

The rules

What you publish keeps to the community guidelines: credit, no copied designs passed off as yours, no photos of children, designs made with the AI designer or Brickify said to be so, and the rest. Publishing means you agree to them. They also say what gets a model or an account taken out.

Something here is yours

Use the Report button on its page, or a takedown request. The same page says how to answer one, if your model was taken out and you think that was a mistake.

The AI designer

It runs on your own Claude plan or your own Anthropic API key, never on ours, so Anthropic’s terms apply to what you send it. What it designs is yours to use like anything you build here.

No promises we cannot keep

Brickworks is free and offered as it is. We work to keep it running and what you share safe, but we cannot promise it never breaks or loses something: keep your own copy of anything that matters (File › Save). We may change, pause or end any part of the site, and take out of it what breaks these terms.
