```text
Set how the booklet prints a step, as the timeline's Booklet row and Build, Booklet page do; one undo step, saved with the model (a turn as LDraw's 0 ROTSTEP, which LPub, LDCad and Studio read). step= a number from booklet_layout or edit_steps. turn= degrees round from the usual angle and tilt= degrees higher, held for the steps after it until turned again, or turn="usual"; zoom= 0.5 to 3 (in on what the step adds); size= 0.6 to 2 on the page; page= "new", "alone" or "auto"; parts= false leaves its parts list off; sign= false leaves off the turn sign a step turned more than 60 degrees round or 45 up or down gets; callout= false builds the sub-assembly the step puts on on pages of its own; per_page= 1, 2, 4 or 0 (as they fit) for the whole booklet; highlight= false leaves the new parts unoutlined in the whole booklet. callout_step= with step= is a step inside the callout that step puts on, from 1: turn= and tilt= pose it apart, from the way the callout faces. The page by hand, one undo step each and alone in a call: place= moves an item of the step's page to x, y (and w, h) in mm on the 269 x 199 page, as dragging it on Lay out the page does (item picture, parts, callout or number; note with index= for words or a picture added, turn= degrees clockwise and opacity= percent turning and fading it; remove=true takes a note off, back=true puts an item back where the booklet lays it); text= puts words on the step's page; arrow= draws an arrow on it, from x1, y1 to x2, y2, bent through bends= [[x, y], ...] if given, red (where a part goes in) or black, 0.45, 0.9 or 1.4 mm wide, or with index= changes, bends or removes one; guide= {axis x (upright) or y (across), at mm} lays a guide on every page, which what is dragged snaps to, never printed (was= moves one, remove=true takes it off). What is placed goes with its step when the pages change. booklet_layout says where each is, and what was moved off what it overlapped. follow=true lays out every later page with as many steps as the page step= is on, as that page is laid out (Apply to later pages), one undo step. Studio's extras: pull= studs (2 when true, 0 or false to put back) draws the step's parts, or {studs, parts: [part ids]} those, pulled out along the way each goes in with a red arrow in; colours= true lists the step's colours under its number (colours_all= true every step's); actual_size= false leaves off the 1:1 guide the booklet gives the first Technic axle or straight beam of each length, true gives every axle and beam of the step one, [part ids] those parts, "auto" as the booklet does it; preview= false leaves the finished sub-assembly off the first of its own pages. A model with no steps of its own is given the worked-out ones first, in the same undo step.
```
