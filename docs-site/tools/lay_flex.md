A motor's cable is laid the same way: `{"kind": "pf_cable", "from": {"brick": 12}, "to": {"brick": 3}}` runs a Power Functions cable from the motor #12's outlet to the battery box #3, in the motor's own 20 cm, its plug on the box's contacts. `pu_cable` puts a Powered Up plug into a hub's port; `9v_wire` runs a 9 V wire (BrickLink's 5306bc lengths) from a 9 V battery box's contacts to a 9 V motor's. From contacts to contacts is an extension wire (8886, 8871, or a 9 V wire); `to` a plug stacks the cable's plug on that plug's top. Two outlets make no cable, and the answer says what to give instead: a box whose contacts have a plug on them says to give that plug. `reversed: true` turns the plug half round, so the motor runs the other way (a Powered Up plug goes in one way only, and a hose has no plug to turn).

A laid cable is an item like a hose: `item` with its name lays it again between new ends, after the model is saved and opened again too, and Reshape in the window reads its ends and offers Turn the plug. `simulate_mechanism` then runs the motor by its cable, as Play does.
