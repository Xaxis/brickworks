```text
The model's build steps, the order the booklet and the timeline follow, as Edit, Build steps changes them. op "list" (the default) gives them with the part numbers in each; "to" puts bricks= in step=, however far, as the person drags them onto a step (a step= past the last makes a new last step of them); "next" or "prev" moves bricks= a step later or earlier, and on from the last step makes a new one; "new" makes a new step of bricks=; "earlier", "later", "merge" (with the next) and "delete" act on step=, a number from the list. A model with no steps of its own is given the worked-out ones first, in the same undo step. "name" gives step= a name= ("Build the cockpit"), said beside its number on the timeline, in Build it and in the booklet; name= "" takes it off. callout_step= with step= names a step inside the callout step= puts on. A sub-model placed more than once has one set of steps: a name given to one of its steps is every copy's. find= lists only the steps that place a part, by its number, name or colour or both ("red 3001", "2x4 brick"), as the timeline's find does; a part in a sub-model is found in the sub-model's own step that puts it on. picture_data= (base64) or picture_url= instead: a photo of a part the person holds, named by photo search (Brickognize, a third party: only the photo is sent, never the model), answered with its top three guesses, the model's parts first, and the first step from from= that places it, with every other one. Brickognize may keep the photo and train on it, and it is for personal, non-commercial use: tell the person so and give agreed=true once they agree, unless they already have in the parts bin.
```

A photo of a part the person holds, from where they have built to: `{"picture_url": "https://example.com/part.jpg", "from": 12, "agreed": true}` answers with Brickognize's top three guesses and the first step from 12 that places the part, with every other step that does. Tell the person first that the photo goes to Brickognize, a third party, as Build it does ([Where does this part go?](/docs/model-pages#where-does-this-go)); without `agreed`, unless they have agreed in the app, it says so and sends nothing.
