```text
Pull a section (a sub-assembly) away from the model the way it comes off, as Build, Break out the section does: up off the studs it stands on, or out to the side it is joined on, clear of the rest by two studs, by whole studs and bricks. One undo step. Work on it there with edit_model as anywhere. put_back=true puts it exactly where it was (one undo step); all=true puts every section back. Saved and exported files always have every section in place.
```
