```text
How big the model should be, worked out before anything is placed. Give the real thing's longest dimension in metres and this answers with what it comes to in studs at each scale builders use, and which one is worth building at. Settle this first: everything after it depends on the proportions, and changing it later means moving the whole model. Give it outline=, the real thing's outline read off a photograph of it (find_reference), and it answers with the sizes to build to: studs and plates at each tenth, each setback where it is and on which side, how tall each tier is, what breaks the outline as masses of their own, and the plan from above.
```
