## Save to a collection {#save}

**Save** on a model's page puts it in your **Favourites**, or in a collection of yours: make a new one there and then. Your collections are on your profile under **Saved**. A collection is public or private; a private one is yours alone.

## A collection's page {#page}

Each public collection has a page at `/c/<id>`, with the site's header and footer: its cover, its name, its owner, its models as cards, and **Follow**. Following a collection puts each model added to it in your feed. A model's page says how many collections it is in.

## The directory {#directory}

[brickworks.diy/collections](/collections) lists every public collection as a card with its cover, name, owner, model count and Follow, newest first or most followed. It is linked from the gallery's tabs and the footer.
