Every LEGO® set in the [library](/library) and every model in the [gallery](/gallery) has the same control on its card: **Own** or **Save** at a tap, and a chevron beside it for all your lists. Your lists, from the account menu, is where you manage them. A public list is a *collection*: it has its own page and is in the directory.

The sets you own are [My collection](/docs/collection): owning a set on the site puts its parts in the app's **Build › My collection…** at once, and the other way round.

## Own and want a set {#own}

1. On a set's card, press **Own**. It shows a check at once, and a note at the foot of the page says it is in the sets you own, with **Undo**.
2. On a set's page, the bar under its name has **Own**, **Want** (your wishlist) and **Add to a list**.
3. Press the chevron (or **Add to a list**) for the panel: **I own it**, with − and + for how many copies, **I want it**, a box for each of your lists, and **New list…** to make one with the set already in it. Each box saves as you tick it and says so. On a phone the panel is a sheet at the foot of the screen.

Own and Want are separate: you can own one copy and want another. Signed out, any of these asks you to sign in (a code by email, no password) and then does what you pressed. A card shows ×2 when you own two, and a dot on its chevron when it is in a list of yours.

## Save a gallery model {#save-card}

A model's card in the gallery has **Save** and the same chevron: **Saved** (your own, nobody else sees it) and a box for each of your lists.

## Your lists {#your-lists}

**Your lists** in the account menu ([brickworks.diy/lists](/lists)) has, on the left (a dropdown on a phone), **I own**, **I want**, **Saved** and every list of yours, and **New list**. A list shows its sets and models as cards, and:

- **Share** (a public list): its link, to copy or send.
- **Edit**: its name, its words, **Public**, its cover (any picture in it), and **Delete list** (with **Undo** for a few seconds).
- **Newest first** or **Your order**: in your order, drag a card or use its arrows; **Undo** puts it back.
- **Select**: tick cards, then **Copy to**, **Move to** or **Take out**.
- **Add sets**: a Rebrickable CSV, a BrickLink XML or a Brickset CSV file, or set numbers typed in ("10497, 6080, 75192-1"). Into **I own**, a file from a site can replace what that site's file brought in before.
- **Download CSV**: the sets, in Rebrickable's columns, for Rebrickable or Brickset.

The wishlist (**I want**) is private until you make it public in **Edit**; then it has a page with a link to give, and is on your profile, but never in the directory.

## The sets you own on your profile {#owned}

On **Your lists › I own**, switch on **Show on my profile**. Your profile then has a **Sets** tab: how many sets, parts, minifigures and themes, the theme you own most, and every set as a card, with a search and filters by theme, decade and size and an order. A filtered view's address can be shared. Switch it off and the tab is gone.

## Save a model from its page {#save}

1. On a model's page, press **Save** (the bookmark beside Like). Signed out, it asks you to sign in first.
2. Under **Save it to**, tick **Favourites** or any collection of yours; each says *public* or *only you*.
3. Or type **A new collection** and press **Make it**. A new collection is private.

Untick it to take the model out again.

![Save on a model's page, open: Save it to, with Favourites marked only you and ticked, your collections each marked public or only you, and a box for A new collection with Make it.](/docs/img/collections-save.webp "Save, on a model's page.")

## Make it public {#public}

On your profile, **Saved** lists your collections. For each: its name, **Public** (tick to let anyone see its page), **Save**, **Delete**, **Its page** and **What is in it**. A private collection is yours alone. **Edit** on [Your lists](#your-lists) does the same, for lists of sets too.

## A collection's page {#page}

Each public collection has a page at `/c/<id>`: its cover, its name, *Collected by* its owner and when it was last added to, its words, its sets and models (the last put in first, or in its owner's order), **Follow**, **Share** (the sheet a model's page has, with the collection's link) and **More** (**Report it**). Following a collection puts each model added to it in [your feed](/docs/profiles#feed). A model's page says how many public collections it is in.

## The directory {#directory}

[brickworks.diy/collections](/collections), the gallery's **Collections** tab, lists every public collection with at least one set or a model in the gallery (never a wishlist). Each card has its cover (up to four of its models' pictures), its name, its owner, how many models and followers, and **Follow**. Order them by **Recently added to** (the default: the one a model went into last first) or **Most followed**. **Search collections by name or builder** finds one by its name, its words or its owner. It is linked from the site's footer too.

![The gallery's Collections tab: the search, the pills Recently added to and Most followed, and collections as cards, each with a cover of four model pictures, its name, its owner, how many models and followers, and Follow.](/docs/img/collections-directory.webp "brickworks.diy/collections.")
