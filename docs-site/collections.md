A collection is a list of models you gather, to build later, to compare, or to show others: a "castles I like" or "my train layout". Save one from any model's page; your collections are on [your profile](/docs/profiles#profile) under **Saved**.

It is not [My collection](/docs/collection), which is the parts and sets you own, in the app's **Build › My collection…**.

## Save a model to one {#save}

1. On a model's page, press **Save** (the bookmark beside Like). Signed out, it asks you to sign in first.
2. Under **Save it to**, tick **Favourites** or any collection of yours; each says *public* or *only you*.
3. Or type **A new collection** and press **Make it**. A new collection is private.

Untick it to take the model out again.

![Save on a model's page, open: Save it to, with Favourites marked only you and ticked, your collections each marked public or only you, and a box for A new collection with Make it.](/docs/img/collections-save.webp "Save, on a model's page.")

## Make it public {#public}

On your profile, **Saved** lists your collections. For each: its name, **Public** (tick to let anyone see its page), **Save**, **Delete**, **Its page** and **What is in it**. A private collection is yours alone.

## A collection's page {#page}

Each public collection has a page at `/c/<id>`: its cover, its name, *Collected by* its owner and when it was last added to, its words, its models (the last put in first), **Follow**, **Share** (the sheet a model's page has, with the collection's link) and **More** (**Report it**). Following a collection puts each model added to it in [your feed](/docs/profiles#feed). A model's page says how many public collections it is in.

## The directory {#directory}

[brickworks.diy/collections](/collections), the gallery's **Collections** tab, lists every public collection with at least one model in the gallery. Each card has its cover (up to four of its models' pictures), its name, its owner, how many models and followers, and **Follow**. Order them by **Recently added to** (the default: the one a model went into last first) or **Most followed**. **Search collections by name or builder** finds one by its name, its words or its owner. It is linked from the site's footer too.

![The gallery's Collections tab: the search, the pills Recently added to and Most followed, and collections as cards, each with a cover of four model pictures, its name, its owner, how many models and followers, and Follow.](/docs/img/collections-directory.webp "brickworks.diy/collections.")
