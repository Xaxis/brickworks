## Share {#share}

Signed in, **File › Share…** (or **Share** on the toolbar) publishes the model on the baseplate. Give it a name, a kind (guessed from its name and tags), tags, a description and a licence, and choose its picture. **Publish** puts it at a link of its own, `brickworks.diy/m/<id>`, and in the [gallery](/docs/gallery). The [guidelines](/guidelines) and [terms](/terms) are beside Publish.

## The Tested mark {#tested}

As a model is shared, Brickworks tests whether a person could build it:

1. **Built in its own steps**, each part goes on where something holds it, and the model stands at every step.
2. **Every part is in a colour it was made in.**
3. **Finished, every part is held** by a stud, a joint or what catches it.
4. **No two parts pass into each other**, by their real shapes.

A model that passes carries the **Tested** mark on its card and page. One that does not tells its owner why, on its page.

## New versions {#versions}

Share again from the same model and the link gets a new version; the page says **Version 2** and keeps the earlier ones under *Older versions*, each at `/m/<id>/v/<n>`. Anyone who copied it sees there is a newer one.

## Remixes and credit {#remix}

**Open in Brickworks** on any model's page copies it into your own models. Share your changed copy and its page says what it was remixed from, and the original lists it under **Remixes**. A model from the LDraw library says who modelled it.

## Take it down {#unpublish}

From the Share panel you can take a shared model down: off its link and out of the gallery. Your saved model is untouched.

## Names and words {#names}

Names, tags and words on the site are checked: abuse is refused in any of them, and names somebody else owns (a brand, a trademark) are refused in titles, tags, credits and handles.
