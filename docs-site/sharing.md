Sharing gives a model a link anyone can open with no account: the model in 3D, its building steps, the booklet and the parts list. It is **File › Share…**, or **Share** on the toolbar, once there are parts on the baseplate. You need to be signed in.

## Share a model {#share}

1. Turn the view to show the model as you want it seen: its pictures are taken from where the view is looking. Its card in the gallery is drawn as a photograph of it on a desk, which adds a second or two to sharing; on a phone it is the model on the page's own ground.
2. **File › Share…**.
3. Give it a **Name**. **About it (shown on its page)** says what it is, up to 1,000 characters; **Tags** help people find it; **Kind** is guessed from the name, the tags and About, and you can change it.
4. Choose a **Licence**: **CC BY 4.0** (anyone may share and change it, crediting you), **CC BY-SA 4.0** (and what they make is shared the same way) or **All rights reserved** (people may build it for themselves and ask you before sharing it).
5. Tick **Show who made it** to put a name on its page: **The name to credit** is your profile's name when you have one.
6. Tick **List it in the gallery** to put it in [the gallery](/docs/gallery) as well. Unticked, only people you send the link to find it.
7. Press **Publish**. Publishing means you agree to the [guidelines](/guidelines). Still building it? Press **Share progress** beside it instead: see [Share progress](#progress).

Its link is `brickworks.diy/m/<id>`. Once it is published the panel shows the link, with **Copy link**, **Copy embed code** (a frame to paste into a page of your own; see [the embed](/docs/model-pages#actions)) and **Open**, and the line under Publish says what was shared: *Shared at this link, 187 parts*, and whether it is listed in the gallery and marked Tested.

![The Share panel once published: Publish now reading Update the link beside Share progress, then the line Shared at this link and listed in the gallery with its Tested mark, the link, Copy link, Copy embed code and Open, and Unpublish.](/docs/img/sharing-published.webp "The panel once the model is published.")

Anyone with the link can download the model's file itself. An email address in its **Author** line, and the folders on your computer that a program wrote into it (a background picture's, a parts folder's), are left out of the shared copy, however it is shared.

Its build steps, its booklet's layout, its motions and what moves in it go with it, so whoever opens the link can step through it, read its booklet and play it. A model with motions of its own has **Opens** in the link box: **Still**, or **Playing** one of its motions, and the link then [opens with that motion playing](/docs/model-pages#play).

The first time you open your model's page after publishing, signed in, your browser records the model turning once round while the page is open: four seconds, a seamless loop, drawn as the page's 3D view draws it, without slowing the page. Its link then turns where it is pasted: Discord, Slack and other sites that play a link's video play the clip where they showed the picture (X keeps its large picture), and the embed plays it too when its address ends in `?autoplay=1`, with **Pause** and **Play** in its corner. Update the link and your next visit records the new version; until then the link shows the picture. The clip is deleted when you unpublish the model or delete your account.

## Post it elsewhere {#post-elsewhere}

On your own model's page, **Share** has **Post it elsewhere**: a kit for each place builders show their models, made in your browser from what the page has published (its picture, the model in 3D for more angles, its parts list) and checked against that place's rules. Every kit's words carry your model's link. Only you see it, signed in, on a model you shared.

![Share's sheet on your own model's page: its link with Copy link, Post it to each network, Embed it in a page, then Post it elsewhere with a button for each of r/legomoc, r/lego, Instagram, LEGO® Ideas, Rebrickable and Flickr.](/docs/img/sharing-post-kits.webp "Share on your own model's page, with Post it elsewhere.")

Press a place and its kit is made in a few seconds; a phone takes longer. It shows its pictures, its words with **Copy** beside each, and that place's rules, each with a link to the rule itself: any it does not keep come first, saying why, and any only you can keep (a group's own etiquette) are said once.

- **r/legomoc** and **r/lego**: a title with its flair (**MOC** for your own design, **SEC** when it is somebody else's, with a credit line from what your model is based on or remixed from), 1 to 20 pictures, and the words to paste as a comment under it. r/lego asks that at most one post in ten is your own; r/legomoc takes no AI-made posts, so a model your assistant made says so plainly.
- **Instagram**: a 1080 × 1350 cover, the model turning in a 1080 × 1920 clip of 15 seconds or less, and a caption with at most five hashtags. Instagram wants the clip as an MP4: Chrome, Edge and Safari record one, and a browser that cannot says so.
- **LEGO® Ideas**: checked against its rules for a project: 200 to 5,000 pieces, at most 15 pictures, no text on the main picture, no box art, and your own idea (a model based on somebody else's design fails). What fails is said. Brickworks is not the LEGO Group: LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group of companies which does not sponsor, authorize or endorse this site.
- **Rebrickable**: the parts list as the CSV Rebrickable imports (part, colour and quantity), and pictures. Its instructions are yours to add: open the booklet in the viewer and save it as a PDF.
- **Flickr**: the largest picture, 2560 pixels across.

On a phone, **Share the files** hands the kit to your phone's own share sheet, where the browser can share files; everywhere, **Save the kit** saves it as one zip, under 50 MB, with a `post.txt` of its words and each rule's page. **Open** goes to the place, to post it there. Brickworks posts nothing anywhere and signs in to none of them: you post it yourself, from your own account.

## Share a model's file from the site {#from-a-file}

A model made in BrickLink Studio, LDCad, MLCad or another LDraw program can be shared without opening it in the app: **Share yours** in [the gallery](/docs/gallery) opens [brickworks.diy/share-model](/share-model).

1. **Choose a file**, or drop it on the page: an LDraw file (`.ldr`, `.mpd` or `.dat`) or a BrickLink Studio file (`.io`). It is read in your browser, and nothing is sent until you share it. A file that is no model is refused, saying why.
2. Its **Name** is read from the file; change it if you like. **What it is**, the **Licence** and **List it in the gallery** are as in the app.
3. Press **Share it**. Signed out, you are asked to sign in first.

The model is sent, its picture is drawn on the page from the model as Brickworks reads it and put on its page, and you arrive on its own page: its parts list counted from the file, the steps the file lays out, and its picture. It counts as one publish, as sharing from the app does; after a lot of sharing in an hour, the page says so before anything is shared, and keeps your file and words while it is open. The form says what was left out of the file (an email address in its Author line, folders on your computer), and anyone with the link can download the rest. The same is left out however a model is shared: from here, from the app, or by your Claude. A Studio file's parts made in Part Designer go with it; Studio's own prints and stickers show as their plain parts. A LEGO® Digital Designer file (`.lxf`) opens in the app instead (**File › Open…**, then **File › Share…**), which turns its parts into LDraw's.

## The Tested mark {#tested}

As a model is shared, Brickworks builds it by its own steps and tests whether a person could:

1. **It stands at every step.**
2. **Every step can be reached**: no part is put down in the air or behind what is already built.
3. **Every part is made in its colour.**
4. **Nothing is loose**: it is one piece, every part held by a stud, a joint or what catches it, and no part passing into another.

A model that passes carries **✓ Tested** on its card and its page. One that does not says which test it failed, to you alone, in the panel and on its page.

## Update it {#versions}

Change the model, then **File › Share…** again from the same model: **Publish** now reads **Update the link**. **What changed? (shown with the new version)** takes up to 280 characters, such as *Fixed the roof; the door opens now*.

The link then shows the new version, and the one before is kept at its own address, `/m/<id>/v/<n>`: *Version 2 is up at this link, and version 1 is kept at its own*. The model's page says **Version 2**, and its **Log** lists every version, the newest first, with what changed in each ([the Log](/docs/model-pages#log)). Someone who built an earlier one and opens Build it again is told *This model changed from step 12*, with **Go to step 12**.

## Share progress {#progress}

A model can be shared while you are still building it, a version at a time, so the people who follow you see it grow. In **File › Share…**, press **Share progress** beside **Publish**: one press publishes this version as Publish does, marked **Work in progress**, listed in the gallery with your name on it. Write what you did in **What changed?** before each one after the first.

![The Share panel after Share progress: Publish now reading Publish as finished beside Share progress, then the line Version 1 is shared as work in progress, in the gallery's Newest and your followers' feeds, kept out of Top and the challenges until you publish it as finished, and that its Talk thread has the post.](/docs/img/sharing-progress.webp "Share progress: this version, shared while it is still being built.")

Each version shared as work in progress goes:

- **to its page**, marked *Work in progress*, and into its [Log](/docs/model-pages#log) with its picture, its note and what changed;
- **to your followers' feeds**: *Ada shared work in progress: Harbour crane*, then *shared progress on Harbour crane: version 2*;
- **to Talk**: the first opens one [Show and tell](/docs/talk#wip) thread by you, with the model's card; each version after is one post in that thread, never a new one, with the version's picture, your note and the line of what changed, as the Log says it. People who follow the thread are told of each post.

While it is work in progress the model is in the gallery's **Newest**, marked so, and never in **Top**, the other sorts, **This week's pick**, a challenge or the shelves on the gallery and the home page.

When it is done, press **Publish as finished** (Publish reads so while it is work in progress), or **Mark it finished** in the Log on its page to call the newest version the finished one. Either says so in its Talk thread and to your followers, and from then the model ranks as any other. Share progress again later starts a new stretch of work in progress in the same thread.

Work in progress follows Talk's rules: its posts count towards Talk's hourly limit, someone who blocked you never sees them, and an account under 13 cannot share progress, as it cannot list a model. If Talk cannot take a post, the version is published all the same and the panel says why.

## Remixes and credit {#remix}

**Open in Brickworks** on any model's page copies it into your own models. Share your changed copy and its page says *Remixed from* the original, and under it [what you changed](/docs/model-pages#remix-changes) from the version you copied, counted as Compare counts it, with **See what changed in 3D**. The original's **Remixes** tab draws yours in [its tree](/docs/model-pages#remix-tree), with what you changed. A model opened from [the LDraw library](/docs/ldraw-library) keeps the name of whoever modelled it, and its page credits them, whoever shares it.

## Take it down {#unpublish}

**Unpublish** in the link box takes the model off its link and out of the gallery; the link then says it is no longer shared. Every version goes with it. Your saved model is untouched, and **Publish again** brings it back at the same link, as version 1.

To leave the gallery but keep the link, untick **List it in the gallery** and press **Update the link**.

## Who can share {#who}

Brickworks accounts are for people 13 and over, and an account marked under 13 can share nothing, by link or in the gallery. An account that has not yet answered the one question Brickworks asks everyone is told to sign in again and answer it before it lists a model. A model Brickworks took out of the gallery can still be shared by link, never listed again.

## Names and words {#names}

Names, tags and words on the site are checked: abuse is refused in any of them, and names somebody else owns (a brand, a trademark) are refused in titles, tags, credits and handles.
