Brickworks is a CAD app for LEGO® bricks. It runs at [brickworks.diy/app](/app) in any recent browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone, and as a desktop app for Linux, macOS and Windows. You place real parts from the LDraw library, check that the model stands and holds, step through how it is built, print a booklet, and share it to the [gallery](/gallery). Claude can design with you, on your own Claude plan or API key.

## What it costs

Building, saving, building together, instructions, renders and share links cost nothing. Designing with Claude uses your own Claude plan or Anthropic API key, which Anthropic bills as it already bills you. Paid plans are on their way: see [Plans and billing](/docs/plans).
