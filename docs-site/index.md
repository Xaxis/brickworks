Brickworks is a CAD app for LEGO® bricks. It runs at [brickworks.diy/app](/app) in any recent browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone, and as a desktop app for Linux, macOS and Windows. You place real parts from the LDraw library, check that the model stands and holds, step through how it is built, print a booklet, and share it to the [gallery](/gallery). Claude can design with you, on your own Claude plan or API key, or another AI on your own key.

## What it costs

Brickworks in your browser is free: the whole web app, AI without a limit, building together, instructions, renders, exports and share links, and up to 25 models kept with your account. AI is never limited on any plan, because you bring your own: its maker bills your plan or key, never Brickworks. Pro, coming soon at $8 a month or $80 a year, adds the desktop app and any number of models kept with your account. See [Plans and billing](/docs/plans).
