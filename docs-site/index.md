Brickworks is a CAD app for LEGO® bricks. It runs at [brickworks.diy/app](/app) in any recent browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone; a desktop app is coming soon. You place real parts from the LDraw library, check that the model stands and holds, step through how it is built, print a booklet, and share it to the [gallery](/gallery). The AI app you already use, Claude, ChatGPT or another, can design with you.

## What it costs

Brickworks is free for personal and educational use: the web app, AI without a limit, building together, instructions, renders, exports and share links. AI is never limited on any plan, because you bring your own: its maker bills your plan or key, never Brickworks. An account keeps up to 500 models today; once Pro opens, at $8 a month or $80 a year, Free keeps 25 and Pro any number, with a licence for commercial use. See [Plans and billing](/docs/plans).
