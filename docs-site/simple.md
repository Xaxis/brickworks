Simple is Brickworks with fewer, bigger controls: a tray of common parts along the foot, one finger to build and two to look round. It is the same studio and the same model, so you can start in Simple and finish in Full, or the other way round.

![Brickworks on a phone in Simple: the header with Simple pressed beside Full, an empty 16 by 16 green plate filling the view, and the tray along the foot: twelve colours in two rows, the tabs Bricks, Plates, Slopes, Tiles and Specials, and six bricks with their pictures, Undo and Redo in the corner.](/docs/img/simple-phone.webp "Simple on a phone: a new model starts on a plate that fills the view.")

## Switch between Simple and Full {#switch}

Tap **Simple** or **Full** in the header, on a phone, a tablet or a computer. Your choice is kept on that device, and Brickworks always starts in Full until you pick Simple.

Switching changes only the controls. Every part, your undo steps, what is selected and your saved file stay as they are, and the view comes back where you left it in Full.

![Brickworks on a computer in Simple: the header with the Brickworks mark and Simple pressed beside Full, the empty plate in the middle of the view, and the tray along the foot with its twelve colours in one row, the five group tabs and six bricks.](/docs/img/simple-desktop.webp "The same Simple on a computer: the mouse is a finger, and Full is one click away.")

## Build with one finger {#build}

1. Tap a colour.
2. Tap a group (**Bricks**, **Plates**, **Slopes**, **Tiles** or **Specials**) to see its six parts.
3. Drag a part out of the tray onto the model. A ghost shows where it will go, a little above your finger so you can see it, and it snaps to the studs.
4. Let go to put it down. Let go over the tray to put nothing down.

You can also tap a part in the tray, then tap the model wherever one should go; tap the part in the tray again to stop.

A new model in Simple starts on a 16 x 16 plate that fills the view. If there is no room where you let go, nothing goes down and Simple says so.

## Move, turn, copy and delete {#edit}

Tap a part on the model to select it. **Turn**, **Copy** and **Delete** come up on the right.

- **Drag** a part to move it. Whatever rests on it comes too, and it snaps to the studs where you let go.
- **Turn** turns it a quarter where it stands. Tapping it twice does the same.
- **Copy** puts a copy beside it, selected, for you to drag where it goes.
- **Delete** takes it off. Or drag it onto the red bin that shows while you move a part.
- Tap a colour to paint the selected part.

Holding a finger still on a part does nothing, so a resting thumb never deletes anything.

![A little red, yellow and blue house with a white roof in Simple on a phone, one roof slope selected, and the round Turn, Copy and Delete buttons up on the right over the tray.](/docs/img/simple-selected.webp "A tap on a part selects it: Turn, Copy and Delete come up beside it.")

## Undo, redo and the view {#view}

- **Undo** and **Redo** are the big round buttons over the tray. Tap with two fingers to undo, and with three to redo.
- Drag with **two fingers** to turn the view, and pinch to zoom. One finger never moves the view, so building never turns it by mistake.
- **Fit**, under the header, frames the whole model again. The view never zooms out so far that the studs get too small to aim at.
- With a mouse, the left button is your finger; drag on empty space to turn the view, and use the wheel to zoom.

## Models from Full {#from-full}

A model you open or build in Full opens whole in Simple. Simple leaves some parts exactly as they are: parts inside a sub-assembly, parts turned at an angle or tilted, and bendy parts such as hoses. It says once how many there are, and again if you tap one. Switch to **Full** to change them.
