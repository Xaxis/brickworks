Talk is where builders on Brickworks talk about anything, not only under one model: show what you built, ask for help with a step, share ideas and talk about the month's challenge. It is at [brickworks.diy/talk](/talk), linked from the top of every page. Anybody can read it; you sign in to write.

## The boards {#boards}

**Latest** is every board, the newest talk first. The boards are:

- **Show and tell**: models you built, photos of real builds and work in progress.
- **Help**: how to build something, the studio, parts and colours.
- **Ideas**: for models, and for Brickworks itself.
- **Challenges**: each month's [challenge](/docs/challenges) has its own thread, started by Brickworks.
- **News**: what is new on Brickworks. Only Brickworks starts a thread here; anybody can reply.

Each thread's row shows the picture of the model it is about, or its challenge's (its cover or its top entry), else the challenge's month, or its board's icon, the faces of who started it and who replied last, and how many replies it has and when the last came. Beside the threads, **On the models** shows the newest comments under the gallery's models, each with the model's picture. The [home page](/docs/gallery#home) shows the newest threads too, under its row of models, once there are three. **Talk** is in the header beside Gallery and Challenges, in a phone's Menu and in the footer's Community.

## Start a thread {#start}

Press **Start a thread**, choose a board, give it a title (3 to 120 characters) and say what you want to say (up to 4,000 characters of plain text). You can add one photo. Brickworks takes out where and when a photo was taken before anybody sees it.

On your own shared model's page, under **Comments**, **Start a Show and tell thread about it** starts one with the model's card at the top. A model has one Show and tell thread; once it has one, its page links to it for everybody.

## Work in progress {#wip}

A model you [share as work in progress](/docs/sharing#progress) keeps its Show and tell thread for you. The first version you share opens it, by you, with the model's card at the top. Each version after is one post in it, never a new thread: *Work in progress, version 2*, your note, what changed as its [Log](/docs/model-pages#log) says it, and the version's own card with its picture, which opens that version. Publishing it as finished, or **Mark it finished** on its page, adds the last post and says it is finished.

Everybody can reply in between, as in any thread. Whoever follows the thread is told of each version; whoever follows you sees each in their [feed](/docs/profiles#feed) too.

![A Show and tell thread kept by a work in progress: the model's card, the first post with version 1's card, a builder's question, then a post a version with its note, what changed and that version's card.](/docs/img/talk-wip.webp "A model shared as work in progress: one thread, one post a version.")

## Ask the builders {#ask}

Stuck on a step? Ask from the studio, and the answers land on the part. Open **Share** in the toolbar (on a phone, in **More**), and under **Ask the builders** say what you want to know: it starts as *How should I build step 12?* for the step you are on. Pick the parts you mean first and they are named too. **Ask in Talk** starts a thread in Help, as you, with your model as it is now: it is published first if it changed since you last did, as Publish would.

The thread shows your model in 3D at that very step, the parts you picked lit, for anybody, signed in or not. A builder who answers opens the 3D, right-clicks a part (or touches and holds it) and chooses **Answer on this part**: their reply says *On the 2 × 2 brick*, and a numbered dot marks the part. Pressing the line turns the 3D to it.

In the studio, with the model open, each new answer on a part lights that part within half a minute, with *New answer on a part in Talk, from …*: **Show** selects it and frames it. The answers are listed with the model's comments on parts. Talk's rules hold for every post: you write from 13, and blocks and reports work as in any thread.

## A thread {#thread}

- **Paste a link** to a model, one version of it (`/m/<id>/v/<n>`), a step of one (open it in the viewer at that step and copy the address), a set or the challenge on Brickworks: it shows as a card with its picture, a version's with that version's. A part number from Rebrickable or BrickLink shows as a part. A link to any other site stays plain words.
- **Write `@` and a handle** to tell somebody: they see it on their [Notifications](/docs/profiles#notifications). Up to three a post.
- **Reply** answers the whole thread; Reply on a post says whom you are answering. Each row of a board has **Reply** too, which opens the box in the row: your reply shows under it and you stay on the board. The box works as [everywhere on Brickworks](/docs/gallery#composer): [[Ctrl]]+[[Enter]] posts, [[Enter]] is a new line, and signed out, **Post** signs you in and then posts what you wrote.
- **Edit** and **Remove** are on your own posts. Removing the first post removes the whole thread.
- **Follow** tells you of every new post in the thread, on your [Notifications](/docs/profiles#notifications) and, where you turned it on, as [this device's notification](/docs/profiles#settings). You follow a thread you started or replied in; press **Following** to stop. Mentions and replies to your own thread reach you either way.

## Search and feeds {#search}

**Search Talk** finds threads by the words in their titles and posts, the start of a word too. Each board has an RSS feed (**RSS feed** under On the models), and so does all of Talk, at `brickworks.diy/talk.rss`.

## Report and block {#report}

**Report** on a post tells the site's owner what is wrong with it. When three people report a post it is hidden until the owner looks; its writer still sees it, marked hidden. **Block** on a post blocks its writer, as on [a profile](/docs/profiles#block): you no longer see their posts anywhere, and if you started the thread, nobody else sees their posts there either. They are not told.

The [community guidelines](/guidelines) apply in Talk as everywhere on Brickworks. People under 13 can read Talk but not write in it.
