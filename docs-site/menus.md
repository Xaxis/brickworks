## Find a command {#find-a-command}
<!-- covers
ui:find a command
-->

Press [[Ctrl+/]], or choose **Find a command…** in the Brickworks menu, and type a few letters of a command's name or of its menu: "same colour", "export picture", "turn left". Each row says the command's key and where it is in the menus. [[↑]] and [[↓]] move, [[Enter]] runs it as its menu would, [[Esc]] puts the box away. A command that cannot be used yet stays in the list, greyed, saying what it waits for, such as "Select a part first". The ones you ran last come first. In the desktop app [[Ctrl+Shift+P]] opens it too.
