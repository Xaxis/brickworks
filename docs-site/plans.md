Brickworks has two plans, **Free** and **Pro**. Pro is coming soon: nothing takes money yet. The [plans page](/plans) is where they are set out, with Pro's price, and it is the word on them; this page says the same.

## Free {#free}

The whole web app, on any computer, tablet or phone:

- the web app, in any browser, with nothing to install;
- **AI without a limit**: you bring your own, so nothing is counted;
- up to **25 models kept with your account**;
- saving to your device and exporting, without a limit;
- the whole LDraw parts library, and its official models of real sets;
- physics: whether it stands, and joints that move;
- booklets to print or save as PDF, renders and exports;
- finding a part from a photo, and parts lists for Rebrickable and BrickLink;
- publishing to the gallery, and challenges;
- Build together, live, by a link.

## Pro {#pro}

*Coming soon*, at $8 a month or $80 a year. Everything in Free, and:

- **any number of models kept with your account**;
- **the desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux, which works offline with every part on disk and does AI exactly as the web app does, and Claude Code besides;
- every version released while you pay is yours to keep, and never locked.

**Tell me when it's ready** on the [plans page](/plans) asks us to write once, when Pro opens.

## AI is never limited {#ai}

On every plan. You bring your own AI: your Claude plan through [the connector](/docs/connector) or [Claude Code](/docs/claude-code), or [your own API key](/docs/ai#api-key), for Claude or [another AI](/docs/ai#other-ais). Its maker bills your plan or key for what it does, never Brickworks, so Brickworks never counts it or pays for it. How it builds is in [How the AI works](/docs/how-ai-works).

## Have an account? {#existing-accounts}

Every account made before 29 October 2026, when the plans page changed, keeps Pro, free and with no end date: the desktop app, every version of it, and any number of models kept with the account. There is nothing to do.

## Education {#education}

Students and teachers can have Pro free, a year at a time, once Pro opens: see the [plans page](/plans) for how.

## What stays free {#stays-free}

Pro takes nothing out of Free. AI is never limited, and saving to your device and exporting never are. Your models are never deleted or locked: on any plan, or none, the app opens, edits and exports everything you made. Every desktop version you download keeps working, and until Pro opens the desktop app is free to download.
