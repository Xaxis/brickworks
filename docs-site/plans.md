Brickworks has two plans, **Free** and **Pro**. Pro is coming soon: nothing takes money yet. The [plans page](/plans) is where they are set out, with Pro's price, and it is the word on them; this page says the same.

## Free {#free}

The web app, on any computer, tablet or phone:

- the web app, in any browser, with nothing to install;
- **10 AI builds a month**, on your own AI plan or key, back on the 1st;
- building by hand without a limit;
- the whole LDraw parts library, and its official models of real sets;
- physics: whether it stands, and joints that move;
- booklets to print or save as PDF, renders and exports;
- finding a part from a photo, and parts lists for Rebrickable and BrickLink;
- publishing to the gallery, and challenges;
- Build together, live, by a link;
- your models kept with your account, up to 500, and every version of a model you share.

## Pro {#pro}

*Coming soon.* Everything in Free, and:

- the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, which works offline with every part on disk;
- every version released while you pay is yours to keep, and never locked;
- **AI builds without a limit**.

**Tell me when it's ready** on the [plans page](/plans) asks us to write once, when Pro opens.

## What an AI build is {#ai-builds}

An AI working on a model in one tab: everything it changes there, until it stops for half an hour, counts as one. When it only looks (searches parts, reads the gallery, views the model), nothing is counted, and building by hand never is. Every AI counts the same way, Claude or another on your own key. More in [What an AI build is](/docs/ai-builds#counted).

## Have an account? {#existing-accounts}

Every account made before 29 October 2026, when the plans page changed, gets Pro, free and with no end date, when Pro opens: the desktop app, every version of it, and AI builds without a limit. There is nothing to do.

## Education {#education}

Students and teachers can have Pro free, a year at a time, once Pro opens: see the [plans page](/plans) for how.

## What stays free {#stays-free}

Pro takes nothing out of Free. Building by hand is never limited, only an AI's building is counted. Your models are never deleted or locked: on any plan, or none, the app opens, edits and exports everything you made. Every desktop version you download keeps working, and until Pro opens the desktop app is free to download.

The AI itself runs on your own plan or key, so its maker bills it, never Brickworks.
