Brickworks has two plans, **Free** and **Pro**. Pro is coming soon: nothing takes money yet. The [plans page](/plans) is where they are set out, with Pro's price, and it is the word on them; this page says the same.

## Free {#free}

The whole of Brickworks, for personal and educational use:

- the web app, in any browser, with nothing to install;
- **the desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux, which works offline with every part on disk, coming soon;
- **AI without a limit**: you bring your own, so nothing is counted;
- up to **25 models kept with your account**;
- saving to your device and exporting, without a limit;
- the whole LDraw parts library, and its official models of real sets;
- physics: whether it stands, and joints that move;
- booklets to print or save as PDF, renders and exports;
- finding a part from a photo, and parts lists for Rebrickable and BrickLink;
- publishing to the gallery, and challenges;
- Build together, live, by a link.

## Pro {#pro}

*Coming soon*, at $8 a month or $80 a year. Everything in Free, and:

- **any number of models kept with your account**;
- **commercial use**: selling instructions, kits, renders or builds made to order. Its terms come with Pro;
- later, a longer history of every model's versions.

30 days free, with no card, when you first reach 25 models. Free for students and teachers.

**Tell me when it's ready** on the [plans page](/plans) asks us to write once, when Pro opens.

## AI is never limited {#ai}

On every plan. You bring your own AI: [the AI app you use](/docs/connect), Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another, through the connector, or [your own Anthropic API key](/docs/ai#api-key); in the desktop app, a key for [another AI](/docs/ai#other-ais) too. Its maker bills your plan or key for what it does, never Brickworks, so Brickworks never counts it or pays for it. How it builds is in [How the AI works](/docs/how-ai-works).

## Have an account? {#existing-accounts}

Every account made before 29 October 2026 keeps Pro, free and with no end date, as the plans page promised then: any number of models kept with the account, and commercial use once its terms are out. There is nothing to do.

## Education {#education}

Students and teachers can have Pro free, a year at a time, once Pro opens: see the [plans page](/plans) for how.

## What stays free {#stays-free}

Pro takes nothing out of Free. AI is never limited, and saving to your device and exporting never are. Your models are never deleted or locked: on any plan, or none, the app opens, edits and exports everything you made. The desktop app, coming soon, will be free too.
