Brickworks has two plans, **Free** and **Pro**. Pro is coming soon, and nothing takes money yet. The [plans page](/plans) sets them out with Pro's price, and it is the word on them; this page says the same.

![The plans page: Free at $0 with Open the app and what it has, and Pro, marked Coming soon, at $80 a year with Tell me when it's ready and what it adds.](/docs/img/plans-page.webp "brickworks.diy/plans, Pro still to come.")

## Free {#free}

The whole of Brickworks, for personal and educational use:

- the web app, in any browser, with nothing to install, and **the desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux, coming soon, free too;
- **AI without a limit**: you bring your own, so nothing is counted;
- up to **25 models kept with your account**, and saving to your device and exporting without a limit;
- the whole LDraw parts library, and its official models of real sets;
- physics: whether it stands, and joints that move;
- booklets to print or save as PDF, renders and exports;
- finding a part from a photo, and parts lists for Rebrickable and BrickLink;
- publishing to the gallery, challenges, and Build together, live, by a link.

## Pro {#pro}

*Coming soon*, at $8 a month or $80 a year (two months free). Everything in Free, and:

- **any number of models kept with your account**;
- **commercial use**: selling instructions, kits, renders or builds made to order, under [Pro's licence](/terms#commercial-use). It covers Brickworks alone: never a LEGO® set's own instructions, other people's models, or the LEGO Group's marks;
- later, a longer history of every model's versions.

30 days free, with no card, when you first reach 25 models. Free for students and teachers.

**Tell me when it's ready**, on the [plans page](/plans), asks us to write to you once, when Pro opens: your email, monthly or yearly, and a line if you like.

## AI is never limited {#ai}

On every plan, because you bring your own: [the AI app you use](/docs/connect), Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another, through the connector; [Claude on your own Anthropic key](/docs/connect#api-key); or [another AI on your own key](/docs/connect#other-ai), from OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, Mistral and others, all in the browser. Its maker bills your plan or key for what it does, never Brickworks, so Brickworks never counts it or pays for it. How it builds is in [How the AI works](/docs/how-ai-works).

## Have an account? {#existing-accounts}

Every account made before 29 October 2026 keeps Pro, free and with no end date, as the plans page promised then: any number of models kept with the account, and commercial use under [its licence](/terms#commercial-use). There is nothing to do: it is on your account when Pro opens.

## Education {#education}

Students and teachers can have Pro free, a year at a time. Once Pro opens, write to support@atypic.ai from your school or university address, or say where you study or teach.

## What stays free {#stays-free}

Pro takes nothing out of Free. AI is never limited, and saving to your device and exporting never are. Your models are never deleted or locked: on any plan, or none, the app opens, edits and exports everything you made. Over 25 kept with your account, every one stays and can be opened, changed and saved; a new one goes there once you are under 25 again.
