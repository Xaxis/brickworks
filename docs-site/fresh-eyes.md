A designer looking at their own build sees what they meant it to be. A stranger sees what it is. **Fresh eyes** is that stranger's look, given to every new design an AI makes (Claude, or another AI on your own key), once, at its first whole save.

## What happens {#what-happens}

1. The AI makes a new design on an empty baseplate and saves it.
2. The save hands back three pictures of the model (a corner, the front and a close look) with your whole request, in your own words.
3. It answers as a judge shown nothing but those: **what the model reads as**, **what reads as a generic box**, and **the three changes a set designer would make first**, the biggest first. It is told of no real set; what the subject needs is its own to say.
4. Then it makes those changes, and goes on.

The answer comes before anything else: until it is given, the next calls wait (and never more than twice, so a session is never stuck).

## When it happens {#when}

- Once a subject, at a new design's first whole save, through the connector, Claude Code, the desktop's port, or an AI on your own key.
- Never for a change to a selection, a sketch, or a model you were already building.
- The look never asks to take away anything your request names.

## Why {#why}

Judged against the same designs without it, designs with fresh eyes were preferred in two of every three verdicts. A separate session asked the same question did no better than the designer's own look, so the eyes are the designing session's own, with Claude Code or without.

> [!TIP]
> You can ask for the same thing yourself at any time: *"look at it with fresh eyes: what does it read as, and what would you change first?"*
