A designer looking at their own build sees what they meant it to be; a stranger sees what it is. **Fresh eyes** is that stranger's look, given to a new design your AI makes, once, at its first whole save. You do nothing: it happens as the AI works.

## What happens {#what-happens}

1. The AI designs a new model on an empty baseplate and saves it.
2. The save hands back three pictures of the model, from a corner, the front and close up, with your whole request in your own words.
3. The AI answers as a judge shown nothing but those: **what the model reads as**, **what reads as a generic box**, and **the three changes a set designer would make first**, the biggest first. It is shown no real set: what the subject needs is its own to say.
4. Then it makes those changes, and goes on.

The answer comes before anything else: until it is given, the next calls wait, and never more than twice, so a design is never stuck.

## When it happens {#when}

- Once a subject, at a new design's first whole save: from your AI app through the connector, from Claude Code, or from another AI on your own key.
- Never for a change to a selection, a sketch, or a model you were already building.
- The look never asks to take away anything your request names.

## Why {#why}

![A chameleon built in bricks without fresh eyes: a boxy green body striped in orange and blue on a brown branch, its tail a flat squared-off spiral and its pink tongue held straight out.](/docs/img/fresh-eyes-without.webp "Without fresh eyes.")

![The same brief designed with fresh eyes: a slimmer chameleon with turret eyes and a crest along its back, its tail curled in a round spiral and its tongue out to a fly, on a twisting branch.](/docs/img/fresh-eyes-with.webp "With fresh eyes: the same brief.")

The same brief, *a chameleon on a twisty branch, catching a fly with its tongue shot out*, designed once without the fresh look and once with it. In our tests a judge shown pairs like these, not told which was which, chose the one with fresh eyes two times in three. A second AI asked the same question did no better than the designer looking again itself, so the eyes are the designer's own.

## One last look {#last-look}

Once the creative pass is in (the few touches of character at the end), the next whole save hands back three new pictures of the model as it stands, with the changes the first look asked for. The AI answers as the same judge: **which of those changes landed**, what it reads as now, and either **done**, when nothing reads as a generic box, or **the one change** it still most needs, which it then makes. Once a subject, and the next calls wait for the answer in the same way.

In the same tests, designs with the last look were chosen about three times in five over the same designs without it, and did a little better against real LEGO® sets.

> [!TIP]
> Ask for the same yourself at any time: *"look at it with fresh eyes: what does it read as, and what would you change first?"* Your AI can look as you do: from up to six sides at once, with parts picked out, at a step, or at a page of the booklet ([what it sees](/docs/connector#tools)).
