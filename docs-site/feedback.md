Something wrong, an idea, or a question: send it from wherever you are. We read every report ourselves, and answer by email and under your report.

## Send feedback {#send}

- **In the app**: **Brickworks › Send feedback…**, in the menu under the mark at the top left.
- **On the site**: **Send feedback** in the footer of every page or in your account menu (your picture, top right), or [/feedback](/feedback).

Choose what it is (something is wrong, an idea, or a question), say what happened or what you would like, and **Send**. Signed out, give your email address so we can answer you.

## Report a bug {#report}

**Help › Report a bug…** ([[Alt+Shift+R]]) opens over what you were doing, with a picture of the window already taken.

![The app's Report a bug dialog: Something is wrong chosen and what happened typed; beside it the window's picture with a red box drawn round the car's front, 1 mark with Clear the marks and Leave the picture out, the version and system ticked, the model and log lines not, and Send report.](/docs/img/feedback-report.webp "Help › Report a bug…, with the spot marked.")

1. Say what went wrong, and what you expected to happen.
2. **Drag on the picture to mark the spot**: each drag draws a red box, up to six. **Clear the marks** takes them off; **Leave the picture out** sends it without one.
3. Above **Send report** it says, in a line, everything that will go. Press it.

It then says the report's number, and **Your reports** opens [your settings page](/settings#feedback), where it is listed. You need to be signed in: your reports, and our answers, are kept with your account.

While the [beta](/docs/beta) is open, **Report a bug** is one press from more places: beside **Account** on the app's top bar; **Report this** on the app's notice when something goes wrong, which sends the app's log lines up to the error, ticked; and the beta bar at the foot of every page of the site, which opens the same report over the page you are on.

## What goes with it {#attached}

Only what you choose, and you see all of it before it goes. **In the app**:

- **a picture of the window**, taken just before the dialog opened, with **Remove the picture** (**Add a picture of the window** puts it back);
- **the app's version and your system**: the version, your system, how it draws, and the window's size;
- **the model you have open, as a file**, so we can open exactly what you were building;
- **the app's last log lines**, which say what it was doing; **Show the log lines** shows every one first.

Send feedback ticks only the picture at first; Report a bug, the picture and the version too.

**On the site**: **Picture of this page** (your browser asks to share this tab, then takes one picture of it) or **Choose a picture…** of your own, PNG or JPEG, either with **Remove the picture**; and **This page and your browser**: the page, the window's size and your browser.

Pictures are made smaller (at most 1600 pixels wide) and drawn again, so nothing of the file goes but the picture.

## Where it stands, and our answer {#yours}

Signed in, every report you sent, from the app or the site, is under **Your reports** on [your settings page](/settings#feedback), newest first, with its number and where it stands: **Received**, **Read**, **Planned**, **Fixed** or **Not planned**. Our answer is emailed to you and shown under the report. Signed out, the answer comes by email only.

![Your reports on the settings page, with Send feedback: three reports, each with its number, its kind and where it stands, Fixed, Planned and Read, its date and what was sent, and our answer under the first.](/docs/img/feedback-yours.webp "Settings › Your reports.")

Signed in, you can send ten reports an hour; signed out, a few.

## The beta hub {#beta-hub}

While the beta is open, its page, [/beta](/beta), is its hub, with **Beta hub** in a tester's account menu and the app's Brickworks menu. It shows what is new this week, from the [changelog](/changelog), how to report a bug, and for a [tester](/docs/beta) what is new to try, what we already know is wrong, and the early features on your account.

While the beta is open, a slim bar floats at the foot of every page of the site: **Open beta**, which opens the hub; **Report a bug**; and **Join**, or once you have joined, that you are a tester. Its × hides it for a day.

Early features reach testers before anyone else, in the app and on the site, and turn on when you sign in. When a feature opens to everyone, it simply stays.

## What is kept {#privacy}

Only the people who make Brickworks read reports. A report is kept with your account, or with your email address when you sent it signed out, and is deleted with your account. A report sent signed out, or one we have closed, is erased a year after it last changed. No IP address is kept. [Privacy](/privacy#feedback) says exactly what is kept and why.
